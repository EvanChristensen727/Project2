# 10.1 File Loading
1) The Campus.cpp loader accepts valid records, reports blank lines, ignores comment lines, and reports lines with too many fields

2) Missing files will make the Campus.cpp loader consider them blank and continue to run smoothly

3) Duplicate buildings with different spellings are treated as duplicates.

4) Unknown references to buildings are treated gracefully in Campus.cpp without crashing, and reversed connections are still seen as duplicates.
